# PANDA — MLZ instrument page (captured 2026-09-09)

Snapshot of the technical data published at <https://mlz-garching.de/panda>,
retrieved 2026-09-09. This is the top of the dossier's source hierarchy
("current official MLZ technical data") and is what the TAVI descriptor's
crystal menu, axis travel and reach figures are taken from. Figures are quoted
as published; commentary is confined to the note at the end.

## Monochromators

| Type | d-spacing | ki without cold source | ki with cold source | Focusing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PG(002) | 3.355 Å | 1.5–4.0 Å⁻¹ | 1.05–4.0 Å⁻¹ | variable horizontal & vertical |
| Cu(111) | 2.08 Å | 1.8–7 Å⁻¹ | 1.8–7 Å⁻¹ | variable horizontal & vertical |
| Si(111) | 3.13 Å | 1.5–5 Å⁻¹ | 1.2–5 Å⁻¹ | fixed vertical, variable horizontal |
| Heusler (polarized) | 3.35 Å | 1.5–4.0 Å⁻¹ | 1.1–4.0 Å⁻¹ | variable vertical |

## Analyzers

- PG(002): "−130° < 2Θ_A < 100°", minimum kf = 1.05 Å⁻¹, variable horizontal focusing.
- Heusler (polarized): same angular and wavevector ranges as PG(002).

## Primary parameters

- Energy transfer: "up to 35 meV" (without cold source); "up to 18 meV" (with cold source).
- Momentum transfer: "up to Q = 7 Å⁻¹ (depending on ki)".
- Sample scattering angle: "5° < 2Θ_S < 125° (moveable beam-stop)".

## Detectors

- 1″ ³He tube (focusing mode).
- 2″ ³He tube (collimated mode).

## Sample environment

- Temperature: "50 mK (dilution insert) up to more than 1300 K".
- Vertical magnetic field: 12 T.

## Multiplexed option

"The multi-detector option BAMBUS, providing 100 q-ΔE-channels simultaneously,
is currently under commissioning."
