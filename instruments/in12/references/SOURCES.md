The January 1 filename records a known publication year where the extraction
does not provide a reliable day. It is not a claim about publication day. The
dossier and the web status check are maintainer-written research, dated by
their capture day.

Snapshots are immutable — add `v02` rather than editing source content.

Not yet obtained: engineering drawings for either crystal assembly, the
mechanical curvature limits and motor sign conventions, and the H144 guide
simulation used for the 2016 upgrade study (Schmalzl 2016 says the guide was
calculated in McStas and the primary spectrometer in SIMRES, but neither model
is public). See [SCIENTIST_REVIEW.md](../SCIENTIST_REVIEW.md).

The v01 dossier's velocity-selector detail (60 blades, 23.9 deg helix, 25 %
resolution, 83 % transmission at 4 A) could NOT be confirmed for the IN12 unit
in the v02 round and should be treated as unsourced.
