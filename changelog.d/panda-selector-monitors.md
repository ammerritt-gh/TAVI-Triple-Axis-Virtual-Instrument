category: minor

PANDA's diagnostic options now include PUMA's monitor suite: 21 monitors from the source to the detector, each switched on in the diagnostic options dialog. The energy monitors use the energy axis only; wavelength monitors are not included yet. A new experimental velocity selector can be switched on from the instrument panel. It is off by default, is not a real PANDA part, and passes only 40 mm of the 138 mm guide-exit height, so about 70% of the beam height is clipped there.
