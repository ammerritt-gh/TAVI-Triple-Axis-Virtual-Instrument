notice: The Fitting dock's Area row is now three intensity rows, and each position has its own Go button.

The Area row is replaced by three intensity rows: Integrated (counts, which depends on the step, the one to read against the plot), Normalised (counts times the scan unit, the same whatever the step, and the value the Area row showed) and Raw range (counts over the fit range, with no peak shape assumed). Each position has its own Go button, the groups sit side by side in a wide dock, Copy results copies the whole fit as a table, and the fit curve stays on the plot when the scan's axes are redrawn mid-scan.
