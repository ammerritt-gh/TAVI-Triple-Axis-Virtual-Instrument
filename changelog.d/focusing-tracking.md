category: minor
notice: Crystal Focusing now has a Track ideal box per radius, a fresh start tracks every radius it can, and a flag on the block says when some radius is not tracking.

Each of the four radii (rhm, rvm, rha, rva) has a Track ideal box that is never greyed out while the radius tracks: ticking it puts the calculated ideal into the field and keeps it following the angles and every scan point, and unticking it, or typing a value, holds the radius. A row that is not tracking says why in a line under it: held with its distance from the ideal in metres, scanned by a numbered command, fixed by the instrument, or no focusing model for the analyzer. A help line under the rows says what a scan does with each. Folded, the block shows its four radii as a two by two with each value and mode, and a flag on its header counts the radii that are held, scanned or without a model. A fresh start and File, Defaults now tick every radius that can track, where they used to hold all four; saved parameters keep their choice, and a file that does not record it loads ticked.
