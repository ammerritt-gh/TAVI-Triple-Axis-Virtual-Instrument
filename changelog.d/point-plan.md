category: major
notice: Breaking change: two scan commands that cannot be honoured together are refused, and force no longer clears them; slit scans now run, in millimetres.

Two scan commands that cannot both be honoured are refused before anything runs, in the Run dialog, on the scan-command box and over the API. The refusal names what owns each quantity, for example "A4 (sample 2θ) is calculated from H in the HKL calculation, so it cannot also be scanned." Force no longer clears these; it still clears the warnings for a one-point scan and for one over 1000 points. A slit gap can now be scanned, in millimetres, on all four instruments but not on the analytic engine. A scan that selects no geometry, such as a plain Run, a curvature scan or a slit scan, holds the motors where the panels show them instead of re-solving them from H, K and L (an API request that patches H, K or L solves the motors from them first). A scan that reads an empty field refuses the Run and names the field. Relative scans preview, run and reload on their absolute values, and a relative radius range that leaves the bender's travel is refused at launch. On PUMA the velocity selector follows the incident energy in direct-motor scans. McStas parameter names are now physical (mono_two_theta_param and so on, with the new mono_theta_param and analyzer_theta_param), and they show in the detector.dat headers. Under a plane lock a direct-motor point runs at the lock's tilts. A step that does not divide the range now stops at the last point inside it, with a warning, instead of running past the end.
