## Deferred on purpose

Four audit ledger entries remain in
`docs/audits/new-instruments-crystal-bending.md`, three left deliberately by
the operator after asking whether the process was earning its keep:

- **Entry 9, geometry authority.** Deferred because discovery found the
  problem is larger than the entry states: PUMA has **three** copies of its
  arm lengths, not two — `instruments/puma/plugin.py:239-242` passes raw
  literals into its descriptor `Geometry(...)`, separate from its own
  `_L1.._L4` at `:135`, while the other three packages pass their constants
  through. A naive registry-wide parity assertion would also break PANDA:
  `instruments/panda/MODEL_STATUS.md:123-126` says its `l1_source_mono = 5.00`
  is a virtual-source coordinate and explicitly *not* the
  source-to-monochromator distance, and `instruments/validation.py:164-168`
  already exempts that field for exactly this reason.
- **Entry 11, module-fixed reporting mismatch.** Deferred because it has
  never been independently reproduced — inherited from a deleted entry's
  carve-out and re-raised by review. Work on it must reproduce it first and
  stop if it does not.
- **Entry 12, test isolation of `config/parameters.json`.** Deferred as the
  change with the widest blast radius: it alters the starting state of every
  test that constructs a controller. Discovery established that there is no
  existing seam for this file (four bare literals); that
  `tests/test_editable_number_format.py:67` pins one of those literals as
  *source text* and will break; and that
  `tests/test_rva_gui_axis_policy.py:301` isolates its own save/load by
  **changing the working directory**, which an absolute override would
  silently bypass. A green suite after such a change proves only that it
  passes with a clean file; the acceptance needs a run against a deliberately
  hostile `config/parameters.json`.
- **Entry 13, the direct-beam energy convention.** Not deferred for effort —
  an open design question awaiting the operator's decision, written up with
  two candidate conventions and a committed reproducer.

Also left, named but not fixed: the `collimation` container shares a
value-validation gap with the other descriptor-driven containers — the API
parses it with a check that it is a dictionary and never checks a slot's
value against its declared allowed set. Not claimed by the audit; no ledger
entry yet.
